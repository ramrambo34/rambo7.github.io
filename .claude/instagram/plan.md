# Instagram plan: 12 Oct to 8 Nov 2026

@rambo_photography_7. Faceless, no voice: every reel is photos + text cards +
music added in Instagram, built with `reels/make_reel.py`.
Nothing is sold, so there are no offer posts. Themes: perspective tips,
the photo journal, memories with friends, the 35AWARDS proof.

Pace: 3 posts a week (2 reels + 1 carousel). The pack's default is 4-5; this is
set lower on purpose so it survives a PhD workload. Consistency beats volume.

Posting time: 7:30pm Taiwan time (assumed audience timezone; change if most
followers are elsewhere). The hook matters far more than the hour.

## Week 1: 12-18 Oct

MON  engage only (15 min)
TUE  7:30pm  REEL      OPINION  #20 Permission     - "You don't need a better camera." Already scripted (reels/01-better-camera).
THU  7:30pm  CAROUSEL  PROOF    #24 The Reveal     - "This is the photo that made Taiwan's top 35." The bee photo, then 4 slides: where it was, where I stood, what I almost missed, what I'd do differently. Facts from Rambo only.
SAT  7:30pm  REEL      TEACH    #2  Negative Cmd   - "Stop shooting everything at eye level." 4 photos: low, high, through something, close-up.
SHOOT THIS WEEK: 3 before/after pairs. Same spot, first the normal shot, then a new angle. Needed for Week 2.

## Week 2: 19-25 Oct

MON  engage only

SUN  7:30pm  REEL      PROOF    #6 The Receipt     - "I posted 12 times this month. Here's what actually worked." Real numbers from Instagram insights only. Run /ig-audit first.

## Stories

3 days a week minimum, 1-3 frames each. Ideas: a poll "low or high angle?" with
two versions of one shot; a question box "what should I photograph this week?";
behind-the-scenes of where a post was taken; share every new post to stories.

## Engagement round: 15 min, before posting

10 accounts, comment with something real (use /ig-comment):
- 5 reach: large photography feature or repost accounts, and Taiwan photography hubs. Comment early.
- 3 peers: other photographers from the 35AWARDS TOP 35 Taiwan list. Same level, most likely to follow back.
- 2 community: local photo-walk groups or the university photography club.
List to be filled in by Rambo (real handles only).
